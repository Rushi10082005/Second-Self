## Introduction to Groq Models
Groq offers a range of AI models for different use cases. The models are compatible with OpenAI and provide features such as text generation, speech to text, and image recognition.
## Key Features
* Text Generation: Generate human-like text based on a given prompt.
* Speech to Text: Transcribe spoken words into text.
* Image Recognition: Identify objects and patterns in images.
## Guides and Resources
* Prompting Guide: Learn how to craft effective prompts for Groq models.
* Model Migration Assistant: Migrate your existing models to Groq with ease.
* Service Tiers: Choose from different service tiers to suit your needs.
