# Introduction to PARA Method
The PARA method is a simple yet effective way to organize your notes and tasks. It consists of four primary buckets: 
* **Projects**: Active tasks with deadlines 
* **Areas**: Ongoing responsibilities without deadlines 
* **Resources**: Reference materials, guides, and topics of interest 
* **Archives**: Inactive items held for future reference
