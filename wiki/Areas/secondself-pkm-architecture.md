## Introduction to SecondSelf PKM
The SecondSelf PKM system is designed to manage personal knowledge effectively. It utilizes the PARA method, which categorizes information into Projects, Areas, Resources, and Archives. 
## Key Components of SecondSelf Architecture
- **Data Storage**: Centralized repository for all notes and documents.
- **Classification System**: Utilizes PARA method for categorization.
- **Search and Retrieval**: Efficient search function for quick access to information.
